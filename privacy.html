Privacy Policy — NestLK

How the NestLK app and website handle your data.

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Short version

NestLK does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts and no sign-ins. The Android app shows ads (banners and occasional full-screen ads) served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. Everything else you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app does

NestLK helps students find rental listings — rooms, boarding places, and houses — near Sri Lankan university campuses. Listings are collected from public sources and shown in one searchable feed. The Android app shows the website at sithumjayasinhexs-dot.github.io/nestlk inside the app.

Data stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the app keeps small data in your device's local storage (localStorage), such as your saved favorite listings and filter settings. This data:

  • contains no personal information — just your own saved items;
  • never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • can be cleared at any time from your browser/app settings.

Network requests

The app fetches the latest listings from the same website host. Standard web requests like these expose your device's IP address to the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) in the same way as visiting any website. We do not log or process this information.

Advertising

The Android app displays ads served by Google AdMob — banner ads and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier) and use it to show you relevant ads. AdMob's data practices are described in Google's partner-sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

The website (sithumjayasinhexs-dot.github.io/nestlk) carries no advertising.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ins — nothing to register.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • No ad trackers on the website.
  • No location data collected by us.
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties by us.

Listing accuracy

Rental listings are collected from public posts and may be outdated or inaccurate. Always contact the poster directly and verify details before making payments or agreements. NestLK is a listing aggregator, not a party to any rental transaction.

Children

NestLK is a general information tool with no user accounts or data collection. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
